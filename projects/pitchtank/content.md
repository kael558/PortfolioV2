# Pitch Tank: When the Crowd Trades Founder Stock Live

## The Problem with Startup Investing

Startup investing is broken. Access is locked behind closed doors. VCs, angels, and insiders get the deals. Everyone else watches from the sidelines. You hear about the next big thing after it's already big. By the time retail investors can participate, the best returns are gone.

What if it didn't have to be that way? What if startup pitching wasn't a one-way show—but a live market where the crowd could invest, in real time, and their conviction shaped the outcome?

That's the idea behind **Pitch Tank**.

## How It Works

Pitch Tank is an event format. Founders pitch on stage. The audience doesn't just watch—they trade. Each attendee gets **$1 million in simulated capital**. As founders pitch, the audience buys and sells "founder stock" in real time. The market moves. You see conviction. You see doubt. It's Wall Street meets Shark Tank.

### The Flow

1. **Founders pitch** — 3 minutes each, standard format
2. **Audience trades** — Live, on their phones, as they listen
3. **Markets move** — Price reflects collective belief
4. **Winners emerge** — Not by judge fiat, but by crowd signal

The result: a new way to discover startups. Not by pitch deck polish. By whether the room would put money where their mouth is.

## Our First Event (October 2025)

We ran the first Pitch Tank in October 2025. We didn't know if it would work. Would people trade? Would it feel gimmicky? Would founders take it seriously?

It **sold out**. Over the course of one night:

- **10,000+ live trades** — The room was active
- **5-star reviews** from founders and investors
- One founder said: _"The room felt like Wall Street meets Shark Tank."_

It worked. The format had legs.

## What Made It Work

- **Low friction**: $1M simulated capital meant no real money at risk. People traded freely.
- **Real-time feedback**: Founders could see how the room reacted. Instant validation (or not).
- **Gamification**: Trading is engaging. It kept the audience present, not on their phones elsewhere.
- **Social proof**: When the market moves, you want to know why. It drove conversation.

## The Tech

Behind the scenes, we needed:

- **Real-time trading engine** — Order matching, price updates, sub-second latency
- **Mobile-first UI** — Everyone trades on their phone
- **Event management** — Check-in, capital allocation, leaderboards

The trading system had to feel instant. Any lag would break the magic. We built on a real-time stack (WebSockets, etc.) and stress-tested with simulated load before the event.

## Where It's Going

Pitch Tank isn't just an event—it's becoming a platform. The vision: an on-chain public market for innovation. Discover startups. Trade. Track. All backed by live market data.

The app is in development. Early contacts get exclusive rewards and investor access. The next live event is coming.
