# OutBoundHQ: Building an All-in-One Outbound Platform at the Hacker House

## The Context

In January 2026, I was at a Hacker House—a residency for builders. The energy was high. People were launching products, building in public, and trying to grow. But there was a common pain: **outbound is fragmented**. You need one tool for finding leads, another for creating content, another for scheduling posts, another for analytics. The workflows don't talk to each other. You end up with a dozen tabs open and a lot of copy-pasting.

A few of us asked: what if there was one platform that did it all? Find leads. Create content. Schedule posts. Track performance. All in one place, at a price that doesn't require a Series A.

We built OutBoundHQ.

## What OutBoundHQ Does

OutBoundHQ is an all-in-one platform for outbound marketing and social growth. It connects your social accounts, generates content with AI, schedules posts, and gives you a unified inbox for engagement—all from a single dashboard.

### Connected Socials

Link Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, and more. One login. One view. No more switching between apps to post or reply. The platform handles the API integrations so you don't have to.

### AI Content Generation

Describe what you want to post—a product launch, a tip, a question for your audience—and the AI generates:
- Post copy
- Captions
- Hashtags (trending and relevant)
- Multiple variations for A/B testing

The system learns your brand voice over time. You're not starting from scratch every time.

### Smart Scheduling

Schedule posts when your audience is most active. The platform suggests optimal times based on your analytics. Set it and forget it—content goes out automatically.

### Deep Analytics

Track reach, engagement, growth, and content performance across all platforms. See what works. Double down on it.

### Unified Inbox

Messages, comments, and DMs from all platforms in one place. Respond faster. Never miss an engagement.

## The Build

We built OutBoundHQ during the Hacker House—roughly two weeks of focused work with a small team. The stack included:
- **Frontend**: React (or similar) for the dashboard
- **Backend**: API integrations with each platform (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.)
- **AI**: LLMs for content generation, with fine-tuning for brand voice
- **Scheduling**: Queue-based job system for timed posts

The hardest part wasn't the tech—it was the platform APIs. Each social network has different rules, rate limits, and approval processes. Instagram's API isn't the same as LinkedIn's. We spent a lot of time on auth flows and webhook handling.
